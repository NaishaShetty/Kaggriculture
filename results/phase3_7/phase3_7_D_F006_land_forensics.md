# Phase 3.7-D: NEW-F-006 Land Threat Investigation

**Primary episode**: 104772378 vs Achille Gohin (5 hands, 10 animals, 4 land quadrants), -$43,127.

## Controlled Ablation

Two synthetic opponents, **hands and animals held constant** (6 hands, 6 animals), **land varied** (1 vs 4 quadrants), tested against Submission B's own unmodified agent across 3 independent seeds:

| Seed | land=1 opponent money | land=4 opponent money | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| 910001 | $23,942 | $17,712 | -$6,230 |
| 910002 | $32,276 | $15,657 | -$16,619 |
| 910003 | $26,158 | $14,549 | -$11,609 |

**Land=4 performed WORSE than land=1 for the opponent in all 3 seeds**, holding labor/animals constant — consistent with Phase 2.3's original finding (F3/F4) that land has negative isolated economic value without proportionally more labor to work it.

## Conclusion: Land Is Not an Independent Threat Driver

Answering the brief's 6 required questions: land is **not** causally beneficial to an opponent in isolation (directly refuted by the ablation); it appears **correlated** with the real high-land losses (Achille Gohin, iBooNDK, Safin, moushun chen, zixma13, the deleted-account game) because ambitious real opponents who expand land also tend to scale animals — land is a byproduct signal of an opponent's overall scale, not a lever in itself. No land-response candidate was built, since the evidence removes the motivation for one.

## Reclassification

**NEW-F-006 is folded into F-001** (animal-driven scaling), not treated as a distinct mechanism requiring its own land-aware response. This is a genuine correction to the prior Phase 3.6 audit's hypothesis, made on new controlled-experiment evidence rather than assumed.
